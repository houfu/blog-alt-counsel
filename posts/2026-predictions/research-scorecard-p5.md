# Research: Prediction 5 since half-time (Jul–Nov 2026) — theatre vs innovation, ASEAN focus

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Query:** Did anything since July 2026 change the verdict on Prediction 5 ("most AI adoption is performative theatre; real innovation in isolated pockets") or the ASEAN-leapfrog sub-bet (bet against Salesforce)? Test Houfu's hunch: "nothing interesting happened."
**Regional Focus:** Singapore/ASEAN first, global second
**Method:** Four parallel research strands (Singapore; ASEAN ex-Singapore; H2 2026 adoption reports; global theatre vs innovation).

## Summary

The hunch is **half right**. *Measured* things didn't happen — almost every development Jul–Nov 2026 is an announcement, access deal, funding round, consultation or speech, and almost every adoption number is vendor-sourced. But plenty happened: ASEAN regulation moved (DEFA signed, Vietnam's high-risk AI list in force, Malaysia's CJ promising AI rules for lawyers), the four AI giants all launched legal verticals, and Singapore's interesting moves were institutional (courts, SAL), not firms. Net: **P5 holds, and the anti-leapfrog bet grades more strongly Houfu's way than at half-time** — ASEAN's H2 activity is rules, risk and training, not adoption. Suggested reframe of the hunch: *"Nothing measured happened. The interesting bits were regulators and courts, not firms."*

## Top 3 findings for the scorecard (cap per research skill)

1. **ASEAN didn't leapfrog — it regulated.** Singapore is a world-level outlier; the rest of the region lags the US; agentic AI is "pilot-rich, readiness-poor."
2. **Singapore: access deals, not outcomes.** NUS Law now has three vendor programmes in five months; SMU joined Legora; IMDA/SAL AIxLegal tracks publish no targets. No Singapore firm published a rollout or measured result. The pockets are institutional: courts disregarding AI-tainted evidence, CJ Menon's graded framework, SAL's LawNet partner API pilot.
3. **Globally, theatre-shaped launches; honest failure data from a few pockets.** Google, OpenAI and Microsoft launched legal verticals by press release; Harvey/Legora growth claims are company-sourced. The "here's what broke" signals came from benchmark builders (Vals), open source (LQ.AI) and one cancelled court pilot whose own evaluation stayed private.

### B. Regional adoption data (H2 2026 reports)

[PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf). *Cleanest evidence for "Singapore standout, rest lagging."* ⚠️ vendor research arm; some country figures via secondary outlets.
